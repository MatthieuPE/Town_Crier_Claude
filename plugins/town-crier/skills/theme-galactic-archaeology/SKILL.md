# Theme: Galactic archaeology

`theme.yaml` (same folder) holds the databases and keywords of stage 1.
This text guides stage 2.

## Targeted science
How the Milky Way formed and evolved, read from its stars: structure, kinematics,
chemistry and ages of the halo, discs and bulge; accreted populations; merger history.

## On theme
- Survey-based studies of Milky Way populations (Gaia, SDSS-V, APOGEE, GALAH, DESI, LAMOST, Rubin).
- Chemodynamics, ages, accreted structures, disc formation, bar and spiral dynamics.

## Off theme
- Single stars or clusters studied only for stellar physics.
- Extragalactic galaxies, unless compared directly with the Milky Way.

## Scoring (impact, category 2 by default)
- 3: new data release, new structure, or a result that changes the accepted picture.
- 2: solid new measurement on a large sample.
- 1: incremental or narrow.
