# Theme: stellar streams

`theme.yaml` (same folder) holds the databases and keywords of stage 1.
This text guides stage 2: whether a candidate is on theme, and how well it fits.

## Targeted science
Stellar streams and tidal tails: their detection, membership, kinematics and chemistry,
their progenitors, and their use as probes of the Galactic potential and of dark matter
(halo shape, subhaloes, stream gaps and perturbations).

## On theme
- New streams or new members, in any survey.
- Stream modelling: orbits, potential fitting, perturbations, gaps, spurs.
- Progenitors and dissolution of globular clusters or dwarf galaxies, when tied to streams.
- Dark matter constraints from streams.

## Off theme
- Cold gas streams in galaxy formation (circumgalactic medium).
- Accretion streams onto stars or black holes.
- Generic Milky Way structure with no stream analysis.

## Scoring (relevance)
- 3: streams are the main subject.
- 2: streams are a substantial part of the analysis.
- 1: streams are mentioned as an application or context only.
