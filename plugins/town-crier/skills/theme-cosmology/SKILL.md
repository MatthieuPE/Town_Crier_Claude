# Theme: cosmology and dark matter

`theme.yaml` (same folder) holds the databases of stage 1. It has no keywords:
every new astro-ph.CO paper is a candidate. This text guides stage 2.

## Targeted science
Cosmological models and parameters, large-scale structure, CMB, gravitational lensing
used for cosmology, dark matter and dark energy.

## On theme
Any astro-ph.CO paper whose main subject is cosmology or dark matter.

## Off theme
Papers cross-listed to astro-ph.CO whose subject is galaxy or stellar astrophysics only.

## Scoring (impact)
- 3: new cosmological constraints from a major survey or data release; a result that
  changes or sharpens a known tension; a first detection.
- 2: solid new measurement or method with clear consequences.
- 1: incremental, or theory without a testable prediction.
