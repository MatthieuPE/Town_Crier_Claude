# Theme: galaxy formation and evolution

`theme.yaml` (same folder) holds the databases and keywords of stage 1.
This text guides stage 2.

## Targeted science
Formation and evolution of galaxies: star formation and quenching, feedback, mergers,
dwarf galaxies, the circumgalactic medium, high-redshift galaxies.

## On theme
- Observations or simulations of galaxy populations or of individual galaxies,
  when they address how galaxies form or evolve.

## Off theme
- Milky Way structure (Galactic archaeology theme).
- Instrument papers with no galaxy-evolution result.

## Scoring (impact)
- 3: first detection, record redshift, large new sample, or a result that challenges models.
- 2: solid new measurement or simulation result.
- 1: incremental.
